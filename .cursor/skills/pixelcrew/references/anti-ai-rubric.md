# PixelCrew — 6-Dimension Anti-AI Rubric

Used by the **Anti-AI Visual Critic** during `/pixelcrew critique` and `/pixelcrew craft` to grade human-level aesthetic quality (Minimum passing score: $\ge 8.5/10.0$).

---

### Rubric Dimensions:

1. **Originality & Soul (Weight: 20%)**:
   - Absence of generic AI template markers (purple gradient mesh blobs, fake sparkle icons).
   - Authentic, bespoke visual archetype tailored to the domain.

2. **Typographic Hierarchy (Weight: 20%)**:
   - Fluid mathematical `clamp()` scales from mobile to desktop.
   - Distinct display serif/mono and geometric sans pairing with intentional weight contrast.

3. **Layout Dynamics & Asymmetry (Weight: 20%)**:
   - Asymmetric Bento grids and dynamic section rhythm.
   - Rejection of monotonous repeating 3-card rows and nested cards-in-cards.

4. **Brand Consistency & Palette (Weight: 15%)**:
   - Harmonious HSL color tokens with structured surface elevation tiers.
   - Intentional dark mode with subtle border glows and high contrast text.

5. **Grounded Copywriting (Weight: 15%)**:
   - Zero cliché marketing fluff (*"Supercharge your workflow", "Unlock AI power"*).
   - Concrete technical specifications, real architecture terms, and verifiable numbers.

6. **Technical Ergonomics & A11y (Weight: 10%)**:
   - WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA contrast compliance, keyboard focus states, zero horizontal overflow.
