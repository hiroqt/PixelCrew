# Anti-AI Slop & Aesthetic Quality Guidelines

## 1. The Forbidden AI Tropes (Strict Negative Constraints)

1. **The Purple/Cyan Mesh Blob**: Never place large, radiant glowing purple, pink, or cyan blurred radial gradients directly behind the central hero heading. Use rich solid obsidian bases, warm architectural stone tones, or crisp geometric grid lines instead.
2. **The Monotonous 3-Card Row**: Never render 3 identical cards with centered rounded icons, a bold subhead, and 2 sentences of generic copy. Use asymmetric Bento grids with varying row/column spans (e.g. 2/3 + 1/3 split, horizontal stat banners, or editorial list layouts).
3. **Overdone Glassmorphism**: Eliminate muddy `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` on every surface. Use crisp 1px solid borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `var(--border-subtle)`), defined background elevations (`#0c0d0e` -> `#16181a`), and real spatial depth.
4. **AI Sparkles and Hype Badges**: Ban floating sparkle emojis (✨, 🪄, 🚀) and pill badges labeled "Powered by Next-Gen AI". Use concise, domain-specific technical labels (e.g. `RFC 9562 UUIDv7`, `Sub-50ms INP`, `Zero-Knowledge Auth`).
5. **Cliché Synthetic Copywriting**: Ban generic phrases:
   - ❌ "Revolutionize your workflow"
   - ❌ "Supercharge your business"
   - ❌ "Unlock next-level productivity"
   - ❌ "Seamlessly integrate and elevate"
   - ✓ Replace with concrete numbers and features: *"Sub-millisecond Redis vector search with automatic cluster failover."*

---

## 2. Enforced Positive Patterns

1. **Intentional Asymmetry**: Offset columns, alternating section density (dense technical specs paired with airy focal quotes).
2. **Expressive Fluid Typography**: Use CSS `clamp()` modular scales and purposeful font pairings (e.g. Syne + JetBrains Mono, Instrument Serif + Plus Jakarta Sans).
3. **Atmospheric Micro-Interactions**: Subtle border glow on hover, precise magnetic button transitions, and smooth entrance reveals.
