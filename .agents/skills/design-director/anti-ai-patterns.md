# Anti-AI Patterns & Design Tropes Catalog

This document catalogues the most pervasive generic AI-generated website patterns and provides direct, actionable human-grade design alternatives.

---

## The 10 Forbidden AI Design Tropes

| # | Forbidden AI Trope ❌ | Why It Fails | Professional Designer Replacement ✓ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Purple/Blue Glowing Mesh Gradient Hero** | Overused by 90% of AI generators; screams synthetic template. | Curated subtle monochrome backgrounds, architectural stone gradients, warm earthy palettes, or ultra-clean high-contrast stark backgrounds. |
| 2 | **Uniform 3-Column Rounded Card Grids** | Boring visual cadence; treats every feature identically regardless of priority. | Asymmetric bento grids, editorial staggered rows, magazine-style quote callouts, large visual showcases paired with micro-specs. |
| 3 | **Excessive Glassmorphism & Frosted Blur** | Illegible, heavy CPU draw, muddy contrast on mobile devices. | Crisp 1px borders, subtle surface elevations (`surface-raised`, `surface-sunken`), authentic high-contrast flat surfaces. |
| 4 | **"Revolutionize / Supercharge Your Workflow" Copy** | Empty corporate buzzwords with zero domain substance. | Concrete, authentic, domain-specific storytelling, real metrics, candid manifesto statements. |
| 5 | **Floating Fake Dashboard Mockup in Hero** | Generic SaaS cliché; conveys nothing real about product value. | Interactive mini-tool, live interactive code block, asymmetric hero typography, or authentic high-fidelity interactive component preview. |
| 6 | **Floating AI Sparkle & Magic Wand Icons** | Cheap AI indicator; diminishes perceived software reliability. | Clean geometric iconography, custom SVG emblems, or precise monospace status badges (`[READY]`, `● LIVE`). |
| 7 | **Every Element Trapped in Rounded Cards** | Creates "nested box syndrome" (cards inside cards inside containers). | Borderless content flowing naturally on purposeful whitespace grids, differentiated by typography size and subtle divider lines. |
| 8 | **Generic "Get Started" / "Try for Free" Buttons Everywhere** | Low conversion intent; lacks contextual motivation. | Context-rich CTAs (e.g., *"Explore Architecture"*, *"Request Private Beta"*, *"View Benchmark Data"*, *"Schedule Technical Briefing"*). |
| 9 | **Decorative Icons at Top of Every Single Card** | Generic visual filler with zero semantic utility. | Typographic numerals (`01.`, `02.`, `03.`), bold pull quotes, metadata labels, or live interactive switches. |
| 10 | **Monotonous Symmetrical Page Flow** | Produces visual numbness; user loses interest after scrolling one fold. | Visual rhythm: alternating wide-screen full-bleed breaks, compact multi-column technical specs, large typographic statements, and dynamic horizontal scroll showcases. |

---

## The Anti-AI Aesthetic Checklist

Before passing any design to the frontend developer, verify:
- [ ] Is there **intentional asymmetry** in at least 2 key sections?
- [ ] Is the headline typography bold, expressive, and distinctive?
- [ ] Are section transitions varied (e.g. borderless transitions, subtle rule dividers, inverted background shifts)?
- [ ] Are all copywriting statements grounded in realistic domain truth rather than generic hype?
- [ ] Is decoration strictly restrained ($\le 10\%$ of canvas area) to let the core content shine?
